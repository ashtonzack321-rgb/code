# Steal An Egg

![[steal-an-egg.webp]]

**Currently the biggest of the three.** It had about 1.81 million players online when the screenshot was taken, with 8.6 billion visits since its July 2026 release. Trackers recorded a peak of around 2.2 million concurrent players in August 2026. 92% like ratio.

## Core loop
**Train speed → run into a biome → grab an egg → escape its guardian → hatch it in your pen → earn money → buy more speed**
- **Treadmills** raise your speed stat. Each biome requires a minimum speed to enter, which acts as a soft gate.
- **Biome guardians** (dinosaurs such as the T-Rex in the thumbnail) chase you while you carry an egg. This is PvE tension, a step beyond Brainrot.
- **Hatching** produces a pet with a rarity and possibly a **mutation** that multiplies its income. There are 100+ eggs and roughly 78+ pets.
- You can still **steal from other players' pens**, so the PvP layer remains.

## What it adds over Brainrot
- **Its own IP (pets and eggs)** instead of meme characters. Egg hatching is the most proven dopamine mechanic on Roblox, from Pet Simulator and Adopt Me.
- **Spatial progression:** biomes give you a sense of exploring, not just waiting at a conveyor.
- **The speed stat** shows growth on screen: you run faster and faster.
- **Mutations** give you a second roll on top of rarity, so players keep chasing better results.

## UI notes (inferred from thumbnail and genre)
- Rainbow-gradient text and huge "$89,121 Sp/s" numbers in the thumbnail are shown in-game as floating income tags.
- Speed counter on the HUD, with a requirement sign at each biome gate.
- Egg-hatch animation: the camera zooms in, the egg shakes, cracks, a rarity flash plays, then the pet is revealed.
- Index/collection book to track pets you haven't found yet, which drives completionism.

## Takeaway
Same skeleton as Brainrot, plus the **egg-hatch gacha**, a **stat-training simulator** and **PvE chases**. Mixing several proven genres is what got it to 1.8M CCU.
