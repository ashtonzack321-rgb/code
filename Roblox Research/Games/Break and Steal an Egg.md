# Break and Steal an Egg

![[break-and-steal-an-egg.webp]]

**The fastest riser of the three.** It launched in September 2026 and has 164 million visits, 217K players online and a **98% like ratio**, the best of the three. Trackers showed a peak of about 30K players early on, so it has grown roughly 7x.

## Core loop
**Train → break → steal → escape → deposit → upgrade**
- **Pickaxe:** eggs have HP and you mine them open. A better pickaxe breaks eggs faster, which gives the game a second upgrade track alongside speed.
- Escape with the animal from inside the egg (the thumbnail shows a sleeping chicken guarding lava eggs, so it's a stealth or wake-up mechanic).
- **Pen space** is a capacity upgrade.
- You need **speed** to unlock new areas.

## What it adds
- **An active verb:** smashing with a pickaxe feels physical and satisfying, not just pressing E. Hits, particles and screen shake make a good game-feel loop.
- **Guardian that wakes up:** the "zzZ" in the thumbnail suggests a stealth-risk mechanic.
- **Community-reward funnel:** players get a free Legendary Sabertooth Egg for joining the ILGames group and verifying in the Shop. This drives group growth, and group members can receive notifications about updates.

## Why 98% liked
- It's a polished take on an already-proven formula, so players know the rules instantly.
- Fair early progression, with free legendaries on offer.
- Clean, high-contrast thumbnail: a smug Roblox avatar, a big diamond pickaxe and glowing lava eggs.

## Takeaway
Being the **third mover** works if you add one new verb (breaking) and are generous early on. A good like ratio helps the algorithm push your game.
