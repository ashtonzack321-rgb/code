# Steal a Brainrot

![[steal-a-brainrot.webp]]

**The originator of the genre.** It has the most visits of the three, around 74 billion. Its CCU has dropped to about 98K now that its clones have taken players. 85% like ratio.

## Core loop
1. **Buy** Brainrot characters as they roll past on a central **conveyor**. The cost scales with rarity.
2. **Place** them on pads in your **base**, where each one earns **$ per second**, even while you're offline.
3. **Steal** Brainrots from other players' unlocked bases. While you carry one you move slower and can't use items, and the owner gets an alert. One hit sends it back to its owner.
4. **Lock** your base. You get about 30 seconds of protection when you join, then a lock button with a cooldown. Each rebirth adds about 10 seconds to the lock.
5. **Rebirth**: costs a cash target plus specific Brainrots. You lose everything but get a cash multiplier, more slots and new gear in the shop. Sources list 15 to 18 rebirth tiers.

## Why it works
- **Italian brainrot memes** (Tung Tung Sahur, Tralalero, etc.): the characters were already viral on TikTok, so the IP came with free recognition.
- **Social conflict:** stealing creates rivalries, revenge and screaming-friends YouTube content.
- **Timed rare spawns:** Legendary about every 5 minutes, Mythic about every 15, and "Brainrot God" or Secret units on special events. Everyone stays to camp the conveyor.
- **The number goes up:** the thumbnail shows "$997,151,167/s" because huge numbers make people click.
- **Small servers (8):** you can always find your base and the people trying to rob it.

## UI notes
- Large cash counter at the top centre, with the per-second rate shown as well.
- Floating BillboardGui above each Brainrot showing its name, rarity colour, $/s and price.
- Base lock timer shown on a button at the base entrance.
- Shop for gear (bats, slap tools, traps). Rebirth panel listing its requirements.
- Rarity colours follow the usual ladder: Common grey, Rare blue, Epic purple, Legendary gold, Mythic red, God/Secret rainbow.

## Monetization
Gamepasses (VIP, 2x cash and similar), developer products such as instant lock and premium gear, and limited-time admin-abuse events.

## Takeaway
Take a meme IP and wrap it in tycoon income, PvP theft and rebirth. Every clone keeps the **conveyor → base → steal → lock → rebirth** skeleton.
