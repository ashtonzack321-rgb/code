# Steal a Beast 🤠 (Game Design Document)

> **One-liner:** Lasso wild beasts as they stampede across the prairie, drag them back to your ranch where they earn cash, and when night falls, raid other players' ranches.

## Same as the hit games (the proven skeleton)
Like [[Steal a Brainrot]], [[Steal An Egg]] and [[Break and Steal an Egg]]:
- You collect creatures that earn **$/s** in your base.
- Rarity ladder plus mutations (gacha).
- Players steal from each other, carrying a stolen creature slows you down, and one hit makes you drop it.
- Rebirth for a multiplier, small servers (8), huge numbers.

## What's different (our twist)
| Twist | Replaces | Why it's better |
|---|---|---|
| 🪢 **Lasso tug-of-war** to catch beasts | Clicking a conveyor or breaking an egg | Skill-based, physical and fun to clip. Rarer beasts pull harder |
| 🌗 **Day/Night raid cycle** | Base lock button | Creates set-piece moments everyone shares: day is for catching, night is for raiding. Builds tension and gives a reason to stay |
| 🐎 **Beasts roam in stampedes** | Static conveyor or eggs | The world feels alive, and players race each other for the same beast |
| 🛡️ **Guard beasts and ranch traps** | Only a lock timer | Defending is a strategy you build, not a timer you wait out |
| 🐣 **Breeding pen** | — | Combine two beasts to get a chance at a rarer one, which gives long-term goals and a reason to keep commons |
