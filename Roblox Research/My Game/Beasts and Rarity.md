## Mutations (multipliers)
Gold ×2 · Diamond ×3 · Neon ×5 · Shadow ×8 (night-only) · Rainbow ×10 · Cosmic ×25 (event)

## Traits (one per beast, random)
- **Aggressive**: works as a guard and knocks back thieves.
- **Lazy**: earns −20% $/s but can't be stolen.
- **Fertile**: breeds 2× faster.
- **Lucky**: +mutation chance for babies.

## Visual style
Blocky voxel or low-poly, cartoonish with exaggerated faces, like the T-Rex and chicken in the reference thumbnails. Every rarity tier gets a particle aura.
