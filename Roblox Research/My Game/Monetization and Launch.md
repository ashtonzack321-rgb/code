## Developer products
- **Night Shield**: your ranch stays closed for one night (high demand right before the siren). 49 R$
- **Golden Lasso (one use)**: guaranteed catch. 25 R$
- **Instant Breed**: 39 R$
- **Lucky Stampede**: boosts luck in your server for 10 minutes, which benefits everyone and gets social reactions. 99 R$
- Cash packs scaled to your income.

## Free funnels
- Join the group and press Notify: free **Golden Stallion** (Legendary).
- Like the game: +10% cash for 1 hour.
- Daily login streak and playtime gifts.

## Launch plan
1. Soft launch with sponsored ads on a small budget. Track D1 retention (aim for 25% or more) and session length (aim for 20 minutes or more).
2. Thumbnail A/B test using 3 variants.
3. Weekly update every Saturday: new biome or beast, plus an **admin-abuse event**.
4. Reach out to small and medium YouTubers with raid-night clips.
