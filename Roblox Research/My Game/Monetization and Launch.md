# Monetization and Launch (v2)

These are modelled on [[Steal An Egg]]'s real prices (researched October 2026).

## Developer products
- **Tame All:** about 160 R$ per batch (Steal An Egg's Grow All)
- **Instant Tame:** about 9 R$ per beast (Steal An Egg's Instant Hatch)
- **Speed packs:** +150K for 79 R$ up to +1B for 2,999 R$
- **Speed multipliers:** cheap tiers starting at a few Robux, as Steal An Egg does
- **Spurs** for small Robux prices (8 / 12 / 19 R$ for the low tiers)
- **Gate Lock refill:** instantly resets your gate-lock cooldown (49 R$). This one is ours and plays on the fear of losing a beast.
- **Golden Rope:** your next lasso can't be knocked loose for 30 s (25 R$)

## Free hooks
- **+10,000 Speed** for liking, favouriting and joining the group (Steal An Egg does the same)
- Index claims give Speed and cash
- **Admin Abuse** every Saturday (speed and luck boosts, rare drops)

## Don't copy
Steal An Egg's original treadmill showed a TikTok-style video feed you watched for rewards. **Roblox delisted the game for it** and now bans feed-for-reward mechanics in kids' experiences. Never add anything like it.

## Launch plan
1. Launch with **areas 1–10**, 7-player servers and the Sundown and Stampede timers.
2. **A new area every Saturday at about 15:00 UTC**, plus Admin Abuse (Steal An Egg's cadence).
3. Thumbnail A/B test: the Inferno Mammoth tow vs a Golden Stallion close-up vs a "STOLEN!" Alpha chase.
4. Clip-friendly moments for YouTube and TikTok: Alpha chases, rope cuts, rustling, and rare-spawn races.
5. Watch D1 retention (aim for 25%+), session length (20+ min) and like ratio (90%+).
