## Weight and mutations
- Every beast rolls a **weight in kg** when it spawns, and it stays fixed. Players hop servers to find heavy ones.
- **Income multiplier = (kg ÷ base kg)^0.617**, capped at 125× the base weight. Heavier beasts also **tow slower** and **tame longer**.
- **Natural mutations** (visible on the coat before you lasso): **Silver ×1.2** (6%), **Golden ×2.5** (4%), **Rainbow ×3.5** (1%).
- **Event mutations:** Dusty ×1.5 (Stampede), Moonlit ×3 (Sundown event), Cosmic ×5 (Admin Abuse). Two can stack.

## Visual style
Blocky voxel or low-poly beasts with big expressive faces (like the T-Rex and chicken in the reference thumbnails). Each rarity gets a particle aura. Alphas are 3× size with a sleeping "zzZ" bubble that switches to red eyes when they wake.
