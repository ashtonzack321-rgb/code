# Monetization

| Type | Examples in genre | Why it sells |
|---|---|---|
| Gamepasses (one-time) | VIP, 2x Cash, +Slots, Auto-collect, faster walk | Permanent power |
| Dev products (repeatable) | Instant base lock, skip rebirth, lucky egg, cash packs | Loss aversion when you're about to be robbed |
| Premium gear | Slap hammer, traps, grapple | PvP edge |
| Limited units | Event-only Secret units, Robux-only eggs | FOMO and status |
| Admin-abuse events | The dev spawns gods live | Spikes in concurrent players and spending |
| Group rewards | Free Sabertooth egg (Break & Steal) | Free funnel that grows the group for notifications |

Engagement-based Premium Payouts also reward **time spent** by Premium players, so session length earns money directly.

## Ethics and rules
Roblox requires that paid random items show their odds, and its policies restrict paid gacha for some regions and ages. Show drop chances in the UI.
