# Marketing and Thumbnails

## Thumbnail formula (all three follow it)
1. **The classic bacon-hair or smug default avatar** stands in for the player.
2. **One hero object**, drawn large: a giant egg, a diamond pickaxe or a rare unit.
3. **An enormous income number** in a thick-stroked font: "$997,151,167/s" or "$89,121Sp/s".
4. **A rarity tag** such as "SECRET" or a rainbow-text "TREX EGG".
5. Bright sky-blue or grass-green background, red arrows and "!!" marks.
6. Glowing effects such as lava cracks or rainbow gradients so it stands out in the grid.

## Naming
"Steal a ___" became a search keyword. Clones put the trend word first so they appear in search: *Steal An Egg*, *Break and Steal an Egg*.

## Growth levers
- **Like ratio and retention** feed Roblox's discovery algorithm. Break & Steal's 98% like ratio is an asset.
- **YouTubers and TikTok:** robbery reactions make good clips.
- **Frequent updates** (weekly new units or biomes) with "UPDATE" in the title.
- **Group plus Notify button:** this is why games offer free rewards for joining.
- **Thumbnail A/B testing:** Roblox's built-in thumbnail personalization and experiments.
